# Brief — Meridian Coffee Roasters

> The operator-authored client brief this site was assembled from. Meridian is the repo's second
> example site (after Northwind): every page's copy should plausibly derive from what's written here.

## What is this?

Meridian Coffee Roasters is a neighbourhood roaster and café. They buy green coffee from a short
list of growers, roast it in small batches on-site, and serve it across the counter the same week.
Whole-bean bags and a small wholesale program for nearby cafés and offices round out the retail
café.

## Audience

Two groups. Walk-in regulars who want a good cup and beans to take home, and wholesale buyers —
café and office managers — deciding where to source beans. Both care about freshness, a consistent
roast, and dealing with the people who actually do the roasting.

## Key messages

- "Roasted where you drink it." — the beans are roasted in the same room they're sold in.
- Small batches, short supply chain: a short list of growers, roasted weekly, never sitting in a
  warehouse.
- People, not a brand: the roaster is behind the counter; wholesale buyers talk to them directly.

## Pages

- **Home** — lead with roasting in-house ("Roasted where you drink it."), then the three things
  that follow from it (sourcing, roasting weekly, serving fresh) as features. CTAs: visit the café
  (contact) / read our story (about). Use a centered hero.
- **About** — the roastery's story: why they roast small and weekly, and what that changes about the
  cup. A short list of what stays constant (sourcing, roast profiles, the counter, wholesale).
- **Contact** — invite both café visitors and wholesale buyers to get in touch; the inquiry form.
  Form on the left.
