# Brief — Northwind Studio

> The operator-authored client brief this site was assembled from. Northwind is the repo's
> example site: every page's copy should plausibly derive from what's written here.

## What is this?

Northwind Studio is a boutique design studio that turns client briefs into shipped marketing
sites. Small senior team, no account-manager layer — the people you brief are the people who
design and build. They sell fixed-scope engagements: a brief goes in, a finished site comes out.

## Audience

Founders and marketing leads at small companies who need a credible site fast and don't want to
manage an agency process. They value a clear price, a firm timeline, and talking directly to the
people doing the work.

## Key messages

- "Design that ships." — the tagline; every engagement ends with something live.
- Fixed scope, fixed price: a brief in, a site out, no change-order spiral.
- Senior people only: the studio is the team, there is no hand-off.

## Pages

- **Home** — lead with the value proposition ("Design that ships."), then how an engagement runs
  (brief → design → launch) as three features. CTAs: start a project (contact) / meet the studio
  (about).
- **About** — the studio's story: why it stays small, how working directly with seniors changes
  the outcome. A short list of working principles.
- **Contact** — invite the visitor to start a project; the inquiry form.
