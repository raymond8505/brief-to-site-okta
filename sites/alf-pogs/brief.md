# Brief — ALF Pogs

> Operator-authored client brief. Every page's copy should plausibly derive from what's written
> here. Tone: balanced — playful and nostalgic, but not a wall of jokes; still plain and specific.

## What is this?

A special-edition collectible set of ALF-themed Pogs — the round cardboard milkcaps kids stacked
and slammed in the 90s — celebrating ALF, the wisecracking alien from the sitcom. The set is a
boxed collection of caps featuring ALF art, packed with a slammer to play (or just to keep sealed
on a shelf). It's a nostalgia piece made properly: real caps, real box, for people who remember
both ALF and Pogs.

## Audience

Grown-up 90s kids and collectors — people who had a Pog stash and watched ALF after school. They
buy on nostalgia and on the quality of the object: they want the real thing, nicely made, not a
cheap cash-in. Some will play with them; most will keep the set boxed.

## Key messages

- "He's back! In Pog form!" — the tagline; the whole hook is ALF returning as a set of Pogs.
- A genuine collectible set, not a gimmick: caps with ALF art, a slammer, and a box worth keeping.
- Two-part nostalgia done right — it lands both the ALF fan and the Pog collector.
- The drop is limited; get on the list so you hear when it's available.

## Pages

- **Home** — lead with the tagline and the hook, then three reasons the set is worth having
  (the ALF art across the caps, the boxed set + slammer, a throwback made properly). CTAs: get on
  the list (contact) / see the set (about).
- **About** — the story of the set: why ALF, why Pogs, and what's in the box. A short list of what
  the set includes and the thinking behind it.
- **Contact** — invite the visitor to get on the list for the drop; a simple interest form.
